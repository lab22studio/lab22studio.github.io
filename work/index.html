intro

lab22 works with manufacturers on the operational layer between their data and their decisions — the commercial pipeline, the program budget, the market map. the part of the business where the right question is already known — but nobody can answer it cleanly.

01

commercial actions tracker

tier 2 supplier · too many relationships, too little visibility.

the commercial team tracked actions, commitments, and follow-ups across personal inboxes and overlapping spreadsheets. nothing was wrong with the data — it just lived in six places and surfaced for management in none.

the work started with the process, not the output. we mapped the commercial cycle, defined the tracking categories, and agreed on what "done" looks like for each action type. then we built a structure that lived inside the team's existing habits. pipeline funnel, win/loss analysis, account health, individual performance. one file. no new software to learn.

the commercial team adopted it because it fit. management adopted it because it talked.

02

program budget dashboard

tier 1 seating supplier · eight programs, three plants, no shared view.

each program manager ran their own workbook. finance ran a consolidated model that was always three weeks behind. program reviews were mostly about whose numbers were right.

a drag-and-drop interface where any program manager loads their standard cost workbook and sees — immediately — where the program stands against budget, by category, by plant, by week. net position, cash flow, tooling recovery, warranty actuals. no manual consolidation. the source file stays as it was; the clarity is new.

program reviews became about decisions, not reconciliation.

03

oem plant intelligence

tier 2 business development · scattered press releases as a strategy.

a business development team was pitching new customers across europe and north america. the question was always the same: which oems and tier 1s are operating near us, what do they build, and where are our access points? the answer lived in scattered press releases, trade databases, and institutional memory.

an interactive map — eu and na coverage — with every major oem and tier 1 plant plotted, filterable by vehicle segment, production type, and proximity to existing accounts. built to the team's specific account targets and updated as the picture changes.

intelligence that moves with the conversation.

04

on continuity

documented. source-accessible. yours to maintain.

everything lab22 delivers is designed to be maintained by your team without us. if the engagement ends, nothing breaks. the logic is readable. the process is written down. any competent analyst can continue from where we stopped.

that's not a selling point — it's a design constraint from the start.
