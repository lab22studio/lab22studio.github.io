privacy

nota · effective 1 june 2026

privacy.

the short version

nota stores your entries on your device and in your own iCloud Drive. lab22 does not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your data. there are no analytics, no advertising, no account system, no server-side storage. your notes are yours.

what nota processes

journal entries — text you write is saved as Markdown files on your device and in your personal iCloud Drive vault (iCloud Drive/lab22/nota/). these files never touch a lab22 server.

location — when you compose an entry, nota may request your approximate location (city-level precision) to tag the entry. the location name is written into the entry’s Markdown file on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

photos — photos you attach to entries are saved as JPEG files alongside the Markdown file in your iCloud Drive vault. they are never uploaded to any nota or lab22 server.

preferences — accent colour, notification time, and other settings are stored in your device’s local UserDefaults. they never leave your device.

what nota does not do

nota contains no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting services, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party tracking of any kind. there is no nota account, no sign-in, and no backend. nota does not know you exist.

data deletion

deleting an entry in nota removes the corresponding Markdown file (and attached photo, if any) from your vault. deleting the app removes all local preferences. entries in iCloud Drive persist until you delete them there directly — that is your data, not ours.

children

nota is not directed at children under 13. we do not knowingly collect any information from children — in fact, we do not collect information from anyone.

changes

if this policy ever changes materially, the effective date above will update and a note will appear on the nota page. given that nota collects nothing, material changes are unlikely.
