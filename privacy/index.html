privacy.

policy

nota stores your entries on your device and in your own iCloud Drive. lab22 does not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your data — there are no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no account system, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no server-side storage of any kind. Your notes are yours, always. Everything nota processes stays local: journal entries are saved as Markdown files in your personal iCloud Drive vault (iCloud Drive/lab22/nota/) and never touch a lab22 server; location, when you allow it, is stored only as a city name inside the entry’s file and is never transmitted anywhere; photos attached to entries are saved as JPEG files in the same vault alongside the Markdown file and are never uploaded; preferences such as accent colour, notification time, and reminder settings are stored in your device’s local UserDefaults and never leave your device. nota contains no third-party tracking of any kind — there is no nota account, no sign-in, and no backend. nota does not know you exist. Deleting an entry in nota removes the corresponding Markdown file and any attached photo from your vault; deleting the app removes all local preferences; entries in iCloud Drive persist until you delete them there directly — that is your data, not ours. nota is not directed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect information from children — in fact, we do not collect information from anyone. If this policy ever changes materially, the effective date above will update and a note will appear on the nota page; given that nota collects nothing, material changes are unlikely. Questions: hello@lab22.studio
