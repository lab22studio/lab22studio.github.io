lab22 is a one-person design studio based in ingolstadt. the work here exists because no off-the-shelf product did what was actually needed — and because the right answer is usually simpler than the software industry wants you to believe. everything lab22 ships is precise, documented, and owned entirely by the people who use it. that principle holds whether the deliverable is an identity, an ios app, or a custom tool — files over apps, process before product, ownership without lock-in.
